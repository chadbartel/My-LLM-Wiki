# Second Brain

An external system that captures, organizes, and recalls information so the biological brain can focus on thinking rather than remembering.

## Definition

A second brain is not a metaphor for AI or a backup memory device. It's literally an external system (digital or analog) that:

1. **Captures** information from various sources
2. **Organizes** that information for later retrieval
3. **Recalls** relevant information when needed

By offloading these three functions, your biological brain is freed up for higher-order thinking: analysis, synthesis, creativity, decision-making.

## Why It Matters

The human brain is excellent at thinking but poor at remembering details. A second brain removes the burden of perfect recall, allowing you to:
- Focus on understanding patterns
- Make connections across domains
- Develop deeper insights
- Work with larger idea spaces

## Key Examples

- **Capture**: Web clipper saves articles as notes
- **Organize**: Wikilinks connect related ideas
- **Recall**: Search and graph view find relevant notes when needed
- **Synthesis**: Combine notes from multiple sources into new insights

## Contrast

**Without a second brain:** Rely on biological memory, limited to short-term recall, forget most of what you read

**With a second brain:** Externalize information, perfect recall, compound knowledge over time
